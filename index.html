📋 About My Project

This model shows an Eco-Smart Home where we use rainwater harvesting, drainage water purification, and renewable energy (solar and wind) to live sustainably. The purified drainage water is reused for watering the garden and fields, saving precious freshwater!

🌧️

Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater is collected from the rooftop and stored in a tank. This water can be used for cleaning, gardening, and other household needs instead of wasting it!

💧

Drainage Water Purification

Dirty drainage water passes through layers of stones, pebbles, coal, seeds, and cotton. These natural filters remove dirt and make the water clean enough for watering fields!

☀️

Solar Panel

The solar panel on the house roof captures sunlight and converts it into electricity. This clean energy powers lights and small appliances without pollution!

💨

Windmill

The windmill uses wind energy to generate electricity. When the wind blows, the blades spin and produce power — another great way to get clean, free energy!

🔧 How Does My Model Work?

1

Rainwater Collection: Rain falls on the house roof and flows into the collection tank through pipes.

2

Drainage Water Purification: Dirty water goes through layers of stones → pebbles → coal → seeds → cotton. Each layer removes more dirt!

3

Clean Water Reuse: The purified water is then used to water the garden, plants, and fields. No water is wasted!

4

Clean Energy: The solar panel and windmill generate electricity from sunlight and wind — no pollution, no bills!

🧠 Amazing Facts!

  • India gets about 1,170 mm of rain every year — but most of it goes to waste! 🌧️
  • Ancient Indians used rainwater harvesting 4,000 years ago in stepwells and tanks! 🏛️
  • One hour of sunlight on Earth can power the whole world for one year! ☀️
  • Windmills have been used for over 1,000 years to pump water and grind grain! 💨
  • Coal and pebbles can filter water naturally — just like how the Earth cleans groundwater! 🪨
